## 1. THE CREATIVE VISION

The intimate wellness space has traditionally suffered from two visual extremes:
1. **Cold, Clinical, and Sterile:** Looks like a hospital procurement portal or prescription pharmacy.
2. **Vulgar, Dark, or Cyberpunk:** Neon pinks, overly literal eroticism, or chaotic dashboards.

BloomCare pioneers a third category: **The Living Digital Sanctuary**.
An ethereal, high-fashion, deeply respectful digital publication infused with tactile 3D interactivity. It feels like stepping into a morning garden enveloped in warm lavender mist, where sunlight filters through prismatic morning dew.

### 2.1 Editorial Scale & Negative Space
* Headlines command respect. The words "BloomCare" and "The Future of Intimate Wellness" are sculpted with timeless serif letterforms.
* Generous negative space gives psychological relief to visitors who may feel anxious or hesitant about exploring intimate health topics.

### 2.2 Volumetric Light & Atmospheric Depth
* Backgrounds are alive with liquid light caustics and subtle aurora veils that shift gently.
* Instead of flat 2D layers, elements exist in defined spatial planes:
  * **Background Plane (Z: 0):** Warm celestial sunrise & morning lilac sky.
  * **Midground Plane (Z: 10-20):** 3D Prismatic Orbital Ring and Lumi Mascot.
  * **Foreground Plane (Z: 30+):** Editorial typography, active dialogue capsules, and floating star pet.

### 2.3 Tactile Prismatic Glass
* UI surfaces mimic hand-blown optical glass with gentle chromatic refraction along high-light angles.
* When interacting with glass capsules, users experience subtle tactile expansion, acoustic resonance, and crystal clarity.

### 2.4 Character Integration: Lumi The Companion
* Lumi (น้องกระต่ายอวกาศ) is the soul of BloomCare.
* Visor shines with friendly glowing capsule eyes (`|  |`).
* Star companion orbits faithfully as an interactive pet.
* Lumi never looks pasted on; environmental lighting wraps around its puffy pearl astronaut suit.

### 2.5 Multi-Sensory Intimacy
* Sight: Prismatic morning mist, pastel skies, high-contrast typography.
* Sound: Crystal chime audio synthesis (`Web Audio API`) and gentle, empathetic Thai voice narration (`Web Speech API: th-TH`).
* Touch/Motion: Fluid Lenis smooth-scrolling and elastic inertia.
